Hello, I'm Boyun Song.

I am a Statistics PhD researcher and AI Research Assistant at the University of Exeter. My research focuses on Gaussian processes, Vecchia approximations, and scalable statistical computing. I develop statistical emulators, deep-learning models, and reproducible data pipelines for agricultural and environmental research, including projects for Defra (the UK Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs).

I previously completed an MSc in Statistics with Distinction at the University of Exeter and a BSc in Statistics at Capital Normal University.
